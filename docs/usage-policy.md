# Usage Policy (Pilot)

Interim policy for the local LLM inference pilot. This is not yet a
supported DoIT service — expectations below will change as the service
formalizes.

## Data: public only

The service has not completed a cybersecurity review. Until it does,
submit only publicly available data: published datasets, public web/KB
content, open-source code without secrets, synthetic or test data.

Everything else is out of scope for now — student records (FERPA),
health data (HIPAA/PHI), PII, export-controlled data, anything under a
data use agreement.

## Availability: no guarantees

The pilot runs on 2 GPUs while expansion hardware is procured. Models
may restart, swap, or go offline without notice.

- Good fit: batch jobs, development, prototyping
- Bad fit: anything user-facing or deadline-critical

## Access: by request

Granted per-person for a named use case. Don't share keys or endpoints —
send people to us instead. The cohort is deliberately small; requests may
be waitlisted purely for capacity reasons.

## Limits: fair-share

No hard per-user caps yet, but usage is metered at the gateway and
per-user keys are rolling out. Coordinate large batch jobs with us in
advance. Expect enforced quotas before access opens more broadly.

## Acceptable use

Standard UW-Madison responsible use policies apply. University research,
instruction, and operational work only.

[01 Access](01-access.md) for the onboarding flow.
